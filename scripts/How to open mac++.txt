How to open mac++ (test build)

1. Drag mac++ into the Applications folder next to it.

2. Open mac++ from Applications. macOS will say it can't check it for
   malicious software, because this test build isn't signed by Apple.
   Click Done (not Move to Trash).

3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
   about mac++, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or
   Touch ID. You only do this once.

4. mac++ has no Dock icon: it lives in the menu bar, and setup opens the
   first time, MacsPane's and then MacsBored's. In setup, click Grant
   Access and switch mac++ on in System Settings › Privacy & Security ›
   Accessibility. MacsPane needs this to move and resize other apps'
   windows; MacsBored needs it to see keys before macOS acts on them and
   to read whether a window is full screen. Later test builds keep this
   permission.

To update: quit mac++ from its menu, drag the new one into Applications
and choose Replace, then open it the same way.

To remove: quit mac++ from its menu (which gives macOS back all its
shortcuts), delete it from Applications, and delete
~/Library/Application Support/MacsPane and
~/Library/Application Support/MacsBored.
